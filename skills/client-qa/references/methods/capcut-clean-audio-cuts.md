# CapCut: clean audio cuts and revisions

Applies to every CapCut job. The lead is very particular about audio cuts.

## Cutting
- Cut a filler only in real silence: check about 10 ms of RMS around the cut point in the raw take.
- If the word runs into the next one, leave it.
- Verify each cut by transcribing a window that includes the sentence before it.
- Keep the voice on its own track and mute the video clips, so overlap transitions only touch the picture.
- "Light Shift" is the preferred transition between talking-head takes.

## Revisions
- Back up the original project first.
- Make the revision in a new, separately named project (copy the folder, new draft id and name). Never overwrite the original.
- Only exception: a client project the lead has said to keep editing in place.

## Past mistakes
- Cuts left "ooo" tails.
- Light Shift over the voice doubled syllables (voice was not on its own track).
