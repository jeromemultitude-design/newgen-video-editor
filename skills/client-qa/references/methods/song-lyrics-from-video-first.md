# Song lyrics from the video first

For any client's song video.

## Rule
- Before any Suno song, write a lyric sheet from the CUT video, not from the script.
- Each line sits against its shot, Adam style: `SHOT n, 0.000-0.000: "words"`.
- Mark pauses and instrumental gaps. Treat it like a VO turned into a song.
- Plan all hooks in the sheet.
- Show it and get a yes. Then ONE Suno generate per video.
- No extra generates or section replaces without asking.

## Why
- Songs made straight from the per-scene script took 6 generates for 2 videos, did not follow the picture, and wasted credits.

## QA checklist
- [ ] Lyric sheet timed to the cut, every line on a shot.
- [ ] Hooks included.
- [ ] Approved before generating.
- [ ] One generate used; any extra was approved.
