# Shorts from a long client video (approved recipe)

Design/architecture clients: use the whole recipe. Other clients: keep the structure, match the graphics to their material.
This won after 4 rejected rounds (static cards, red circles and pills, B-roll off the voice, real room photos).

## Story
- The hook opens a question and moves from frame 1 with a hero asset.
- A heavily blurred tease with a big "?" is OK. Nothing sharper.
- Titles are open loops ("can you spot it?", "clue 1"). The middle is a clue game.
- Never show real photos or finished renders, even if the brief asks. Flag it.

## Picture on the word
- List each noun with its word timecode. That frame must show it.
- One continuous camera on the plan, arriving on each word.
- No footage? Make it literal on the drawing: storage = wash the wardrobes, footprint = pen line round the house, "disappear" = erase, "hidden" = a wash covers it.

## Graphics
- 1080x1920, warm paper #f3f0e8, full-width band.
- Title above; one caption phrase below with one red key word.
- Client's drawn furniture. Watercolour washes (terracotta, ochre, blue; multiply; noisy edges).
- Pen lettering (Ink Free) off drawn lines. Hand-drawn lens, paint-splash transitions, red pen.
- No pills, boxes, shadows or stock circles unless the client asks.

## Finish
- Client logo outro if the client has one.
- Voice plus quiet music, -14 LUFS. H.264, 30 fps.
- QC including a per-frame flash scan (no white/black flash frames).
