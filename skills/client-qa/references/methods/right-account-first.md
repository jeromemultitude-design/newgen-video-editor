# Right account first, one client at a time

## Accounts and browser profiles
- Each client's tools (generators, voice, Drive) live in that client's account. Never use one client's account for another client's job.
- Before any action, read which account is signed in on the page.
- When switching to a browser profile, do the switch on its own first and read the result. If the right profile is not connected, STOP and ask the lead to open it. Never batch the switch with other actions: a failed switch let a navigate run in the wrong profile.
- Never try other browsers or profiles on your own. If the login isn't reachable, ask once at the start.
- The lead signs in. Never sign in yourself.
- Read an account's history before uploading anything to it (a voice sample once went into the wrong account's clone form).
- Never send anything to a client yourself.

## Stay on the client in front of you
- In one client's channel, talk only about that client. Don't relay updates about other clients' jobs unless it blocks this work or the lead asks. The lead watches each job directly; relays are noise.
