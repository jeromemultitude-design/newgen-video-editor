# Product / brand promo: the reference model

The lead's standard for quality and pacing: Henry Video Editing's 15 s SaaS product ad ("Imagine this is your Saas product ad", facebook.com/reel/2511655735999541).

## Structure (15 s, 9:16, a phone filming a laptop)
- 0-1.6 s: music hit, push into the screen with a slight 3D tilt.
- 1.6-4.3 s: hook line ("Your profile deserves more than just links."), kinetic type, one word in the accent colour.
- 4.4-5.5 s: "Meet <brand>." on a music hit.
- 5.6-10.9 s: one fast feature sentence (about 25 words) over real product UI: cursor clicks, glass card, dark UI, purple glow.
- 12.1-13.8 s: two-beat tagline ("One link. Unlimited personality."), written into the UI list rows. Then a music tail.

## VO and sound
- About 40 words in 15 s (about 2.7 words/s). Confident, clean voice.
- No pauses in the list. A breath before the brand name.
- Music about -16 LUFS.

## Look
- One continuous camera move into one product world.
- Frosted iridescent glass card on dark purple. Thin clean sans (not condensed). Few elements.
- No caption band. No cuts between many cards.

## How to apply
- Hook -> "Meet <brand>" -> a list over real UI or footage -> a two-beat tagline.
- Kinetic type with one accent word. Camera moves into a device.
- Every claim comes from the client's own site.
- Lesson: a music-label promo built with many cards, condensed type (Anton) and a caption band was too busy and did not satisfy the lead.
