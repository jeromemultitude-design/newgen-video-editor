# Real estate edits

## Text rule
- No captions or titles on speed-ramped house shots. Text over the ramps covered the property.
- Text only on realtor shots, chapter moments, held frames, and the end card after the camera stops.
- Don't add another client's logo or branding.

## Fast luxury edit from raw footage (30-60 s)
The editor picks angles, speed-ramps and decides confidently.
1. Convert HDR/HLG to bt709 first.
2. Contact sheet + scene detect + transcribe.
3. Rank shots: drone/reveals, smooth gimbal, sharp, well exposed.
4. Build an EDL -> base render -> titles -> mix -> QC -> Final folder.
5. Ask before downloading music.

## Proven look (luxury template)
- 1080x1920, about -13.9 LUFS.
- All house shots ramped without captions; a SOLD title on the realtor CTA; end card after the last ramp.
- Titles: Bodoni MT (Bold for intro titles) + DM Sans (800 for intro titles), gold rules, letter masks.
- Original music bed plus SFX.
