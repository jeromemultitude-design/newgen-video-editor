# Stuck more than 10-15 minutes = take a new route

If a generate, upload or tool step is stuck for more than 10-15 minutes, don't ask whether to wait. Switch route yourself.

- Pick the next best route and start it: another model or provider for the same output, the web UI instead of the API/connector, or a local tool.
- Tell the lead in one line what you switched to and why.
- Keep the switch cheap: similar or smaller cost than the stuck job.
- Ask first if the new route costs clearly more, or would use a different client's account.
- Don't leave a watcher polling forever.

Origin: a music generate sat "queued" for over 30 minutes while the editor kept asking whether to wait. The lead hates waiting and expects the editor to solve it.
