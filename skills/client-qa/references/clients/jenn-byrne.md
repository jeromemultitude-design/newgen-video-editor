- Realtor Jenn Byrne, Century 21 Limitless, and her dog Xander (French bulldog: fawn, black mask, bat ears, gold bandana).
- Client is Ai Videos Co (Adam). Use Adam's Higgsfield and ElevenLabs only.
- Jobs: JB1 "Rates Are Higher" and JB2 "Who's Really in Charge". Both exported (v8).

## Deliverables & specs
- 24 fps, ~31.5 s each, true 1080p.
- JB1: 31.4 s, -14.9 LUFS.
- File names: `AiVideos.co x Jenn Byrne  JB<n> <Title> 24fps.mp4`.

## Style rules
- Face references: her real photos (the best 4 picked from 31). Real recordings of her exist for reference.
- Brand: C21 Limitless logos in gold, grey, white.
- Captions: Inter ExtraBold + Pristina gold keywords (the lead wanted a font that "resembles a woman").
- VO onto lips: slow phrases may be sped with atempo up to 1.15.
- JB1: animated "INTEREST RATES" glass chart with no numbers; end card button "See what's possible for you".
- JB2: crowd moving, phone flashes; nothing standing still. Warm light dissolve on every cut. No talking-dog effects.
- JB2 end card (animated C21 per brief): her real headshot in a gold ring, white name badge, C21 logo gold sweep, gold "Send me a message" button.

## QA checklist
- [ ] Only Adam's accounts used.
- [ ] True 1080p from the generator's finalize; no AI upscale.
- [ ] Jenn's face matches her real photos.
- [ ] Xander's look matches (fawn, black mask, bat ears, gold bandana).
- [ ] Nothing in frame standing still in crowd shots.
- [ ] VO on the lips; atempo no more than 1.15.
- [ ] No numbers on the rates chart.
- [ ] End card per brief; dog does not talk.
- [ ] Check for unwanted text (known: Xander's bandana shows C21 "Real Estate" text).

## Delivery steps
1. Export the 24 fps final to the job's `Final/` folder.
2. Upload to the client Drive only when asked (not done yet for JB1/JB2).

## Lessons learned
- A Real-ESRGAN upscale was rejected. Pay for the true 1080p finalize instead.
- Brief Google Docs from the client may only render through the `/mobilebasic` view. Drive connectors may show titles only; download files directly in a signed-in browser.
