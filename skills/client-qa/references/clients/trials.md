# Trial clients

Trials are paid or free tests from prospects. Ask at the plan stage. Show the first QC video the same day. A delivered trial's folder gets " - Delivered" added to its name.

## Cross-trial lessons
- Transcribe every raw clip first. Plan script by script from the voice.
- QC is always a video on the review timeline page, never a still or a description.
- Check deliverables against the client's instructions PDF before export and submission. List every gap to the lead. (Josiah Krol went in without covers, thumbnail and artwork and got no reply.)
- Voice crackle came from the ffmpeg de-esser. Room echo ("cave") only goes away with Adobe Enhance Speech.
- Over-processed voice sounds "tin can". Light denoise plus a faint room tone is what gets approved (unless the client's reference is dry studio voice).
- Check voice placements for overlaps before every render.
- Follow the client's reference style closely: font, effects, speed-ramp shape.
- Fonts that won: Montserrat for spoken-word kinetic type; Imperial Script for luxury headlines (gold, glow, underline); Gloock caps for highlight words.
- Real estate edits: no captions on speed ramps.
- Join cuts in true silence (measure RMS; don't trust transcript word times).
- Check every cut frame: renderers can hold the old caption a frame after a cut.
- Never use Higgsfield except on Ai Videos Co jobs.
- "Export" means keep the final and delete all drafts.
- A step stuck more than 10-15 min: switch route and say so in one line.

**Deliverables & specs:**
- VO/doctor ad, 64 s.
- "The Pool" drama (Script 2 v5), 67.8 s; 8 Gemini Omni Flash 1.1 clips (~$4.41) + ElevenLabs score.

**Style rules:**
- Audience: white women 60+ only. Skin shots show SK only.
- No 0.5 s cuts, no small text, soft music.
- Drama: keep the AI clips' own cuts and lip-sync; dissolves only at joins. 1 s water ripple on a held opening frame. Soft-gold key words. FORA logo black zoom-in banner in the subtitle area. 3D-clip cutaways with calm crossfades, never back to back. Mute wrong-mouth lines rather than cut.
- VO ad: raw-iPhone image-to-video look (the client's reference), 0.35 s crossfades, an infographic on every beat, lip-synced doctor, the real 60-day seal, padded CTA.

**QA checklist:**
- [ ] Script word for word from the client doc.
- [ ] Only women 60+; SK-only skin.
- [ ] No cut shorter than 0.5 s; no small text.
- [ ] Listen for AI flubs and wrong-mouth lines.
- [ ] Real 60-day seal used.

**Lessons:**
- GenStudio: Seedance with a start frame needs "adaptive" and bills ~40% over the quote. Omni 1080p costs the same as 720p. Clips download from the page's video source. Its built-in ElevenLabs was broken; use an approved account.
